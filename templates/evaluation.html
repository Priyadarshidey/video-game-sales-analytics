Metric definitions

MetricFormulaInterpretation
MAEmean(|y − ŷ|)Typical error in million units. Lower is better; robust to outliers.
MSEmean((y − ŷ)²)Squares errors, so a few huge misses (blockbusters) dominate. Lower is better.
RMSE√MSESame units as sales. Lower is better.
R²1 − SS_res / SS_totFraction of variance explained. Higher is better; 0 = no better than predicting the mean; negative = worse.
MAPEmean(|y − ŷ| / |y|) × 100 over y ≠ 0Average error as a percentage. Zero actual values are excluded to avoid division by zero. Lower is better; inflated when actual values are tiny.

Reading the results

  • The target is heavily skewed: most titles sell well under a million units, a handful sell tens of millions. Squared-error metrics (MSE, RMSE, R²) are dominated by those few blockbusters, whereas MAE and MAPE reflect the typical title. The two groups can therefore favour different models.
  • R² is {{ 'low' if meta.linear_metrics.R2 < 0.5 and meta.random_forest_metrics.R2 < 0.5 else 'moderate/high' }} for both models: with only platform, genre, publisher and year as inputs, a large part of the variation in sales is unexplained.
  • Metrics are computed on the raw model outputs (negative linear predictions are not clipped in evaluation).
